- **Product fit:** a sealed welded-seam coated-nylon fiber-core institutional unit, not the
  innerspring build quoted for JBSA Bldg 6578 / CRTC. Operator confirmed 2026-09-15 that CSP
  can produce it: welded sealed cover, TB 129 test path, fiber core all in range.

  also asked (CSP recommended 7–8").
