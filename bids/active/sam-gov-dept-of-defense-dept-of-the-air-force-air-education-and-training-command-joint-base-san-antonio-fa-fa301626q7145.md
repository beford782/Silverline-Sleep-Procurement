## 2. Fit assessment

- **Product fit:** a sealed welded-seam coated-nylon fiber-core institutional unit, not the
  innerspring build quoted for JBSA Bldg 6578 / CRTC. Operator confirmed 2026-09-15 that CSP
  can produce it: welded sealed cover, TB 129 test path, fiber core all in range.
- **Spec wording reads as vendor catalog copy** ("Silver Ion Technology", "Max-life fiber
  core", "Zero thread seams"); expect a brand-name-or-equal RFQ. Equal is achievable if the
  three gates above hold.
- **Open spec question:** silver ion specifically vs. an equivalent medical-grade antimicrobial
  cover (asked 09-15; affects fabric sourcing and price). Thickness preference within 6–8"
  also asked (CSP recommended 7–8").
- **Eligibility:** SAM Active, UEI XF73FG8CVMX1, CAGE 22NH3, small business under 337910.
  No blocker.
