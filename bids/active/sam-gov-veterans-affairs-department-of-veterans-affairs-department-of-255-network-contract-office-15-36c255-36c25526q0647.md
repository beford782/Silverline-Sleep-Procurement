## 1. Scope summary

Firm-fixed-price commercial RFQ to replace end-of-life behavioral-health
mattresses at the Marion VA **Residential Rehabilitation Treatment Program
(RRTP)**, including delivery, installation, and removal/disposal of the old
mattresses.

| CLIN | Item | Qty | Unit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0001 | `NP TRIO BH 35 x 82 w/ defined perimeter` — new patient mattresses (local stock no. 0010TF03581BHD) | 20 | EA |
| 0002 | Pick up and dispose of all replaced mattresses (local stock no. DISPOSAL) | 1 | EA |

Minimum technical specification (Section B), which is the real set of
salient characteristics:

- Suitable for a behavioral health environment
- **RF-welded seams** so internal components cannot be accessed
- **Full-length defined perimeter** (security + fall-risk reduction)
- Compatible with BH platforms / VA-owned frames
- Max **35 in** wide × **82 in** long; min **500 lb** weight capacity
- **Halogen-free** fire barrier meeting applicable fire safety regulation
- Non-skid bottom cover; **4-way stretch** top cover
- Foot-end vents; **Core Shield** layer
- **Copper and gel-infused** foam for microclimate control
- **Three distinct foam layers** in head, torso, and heel sections

Contractor also manages all packaging/installation waste, provides the
manufacturer's standard warranty with documentation delivered per mattress,
and coordinates install with RRTP staff under VA safety/security/infection
control policy.

Source: notice + SF 1449 package on SAM
(<https://sam.gov/workspace/contract/opp/ab33baa29fd641bbb6befa2d6c62f686/view>).

## 2. Fit assessment

- **Product fit:** core behavioral-health mattress work. NAICS 337910 is
  CSP's own code and CSP is the manufacturer, not a reseller.
- **Continuity:** this is the **follow-on to 36C25526Q0607**, the Sources
  Sought CSP answered by the 2026-08-19 deadline — same contracting officer
  (Jon Lutmer), same Marion RRTP requirement, same spec language. The
  capability response is already in this CO's hands; this quote should read
  as the continuation of it.
- **Brand exposure — the main risk.** CLIN 0001 names a specific commercial
  model (`NP TRIO BH`) plus a VA local stock number, and the RFQ contains
  **no brand-name-or-equal provision**. The award basis is nevertheless
  technical requirement + past performance + price (FAR 52.212-2), so an
  equal alternative is evaluable — but only if the quote maps CSP's model
  to **each** bullet in Section B explicitly and states plainly where it
  differs. Terms like "Core Shield layer" read as vendor-proprietary
  vocabulary; answer them by construction and function, not by name.
- **Eligibility:** SAM **Active** (UEI XF73FG8CVMX1, CAGE 22NH3, expires
  2027-07-07). Offeror must be SAM-registered at submission *and* at award,
  and verified in SBA Small Business Search if applicable.
- **Scale:** 20 units. Small-dollar, so it will not move the win_score
  (17), but it is a live, closable requirement with a warm POC and a
  reference-quality VA past-performance citation if won.

## 3. Evaluation / award

Firm-fixed-price, evaluated on **technical requirement, past performance,
and price** (FAR 52.212-2). Government intends to award **without
discussions**, so the initial quote must carry best terms. It reserves the
right to accept other than the lowest offer.

## 5. Decision rationale

**Recommend BID.** Core product, total small-business set-aside under CSP's
own NAICS, a contracting officer who already holds CSP's capability
statement, and a 4-day runway with the full spec in hand and no portal
registration to clear. The order is small, but it converts an existing
Sources Sought touch into a real quote and, if won, produces a VA
past-performance reference for the larger recurring VHA mattress channel
already tracked in Lead Radar.

The one genuine risk is the named model. If CSP cannot meet a specific
salient characteristic (RF-weld vs inverted sealed seam; copper/gel foam;
three-layer head/torso/heel), say so in the technical response rather than
staying silent — award is not lowest-price-technically-acceptable, and a
candid deviation is survivable where an unstated one is not.
