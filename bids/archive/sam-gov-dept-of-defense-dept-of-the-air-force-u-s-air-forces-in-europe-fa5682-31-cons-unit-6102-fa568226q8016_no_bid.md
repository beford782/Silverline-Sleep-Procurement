## 1. Summary

Combined synopsis/solicitation for removal, disposal, delivery, and
installation of 102 sets of dormitory furniture at Building 1424,
Aviano Air Base, Italy. Fourth USAFE Aviano dormitory-furniture
sibling after FA568226QA053, FA568226Q8018, and FA568226QA052, all
no-bid on identical grounds.

## 2. Disqualifying factors

- **Primary reason:** Out-of-region — delivery and installation at
  Aviano AB, Italy, far outside the TX/OK/LA/MS/AR/NM service
  geography.
- **Contributing reasons:** Furniture/casegoods scope (NAICS 449110),
  not mattress/bedding product; removal/disposal/installation service
  components; SAM registration not yet Active at decision time.

## 3. What would change a future bid/no-bid call

- Nothing at this scope — OCONUS furniture installation is
  categorically outside the vendor's lane. A domestic
  mattress-only line item from the same buyer would be re-triaged
  on its own merits.

## 4. Lessons captured

- USAFE/Aviano dormitory-furniture postings recur under FA5682
  solicitation numbers; they can be fast-tracked to no-bid by
  matching the prior siblings.
- Keep `overseas` in the scoring caution vocabulary; OCONUS place of
  performance should surface at triage, not at drafting.
