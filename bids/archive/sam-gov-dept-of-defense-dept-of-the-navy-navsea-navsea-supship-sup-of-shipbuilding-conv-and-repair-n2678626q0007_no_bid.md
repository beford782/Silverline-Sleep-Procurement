## 1. Summary

Combined synopsis/solicitation posted 2026-09-02, due 2026-09-17, NAICS
337121 / PSC 7210, Total Small Business Set-Aside, firm-fixed-price purchase
order. Surfaced by the 2026-09-03 SAM ingest (PR #176) at fit 80 and landed
directly in the **active** pipeline.

## 2. Disqualifying factors

- **Not a mattress or bedding buy.** The requirement is custom-fitted,
  heavy-duty **marine-grade Naugahyde vinyl table and seating covers** for
  the Wardroom, Chief Petty Officer Mess, and Crews Mess of the newly built
  guided-missile destroyer USS Patrick Gallagher (DDG-127).
- **Why it scored 80:** the fit score came from PSC 7210 (Household
  Furnishings) plus NAICS 337121 (Upholstered Household Furniture
  Manufacturing) alone. Neither the title nor the description contains any
  mattress or bedding content. This is marine upholstery, outside CSP's
  product line and outside its installation footprint.

## 3. Decision

**NO-BID.** Archived at triage on the day it arrived rather than left in the
active pipeline, where it would have consumed a slot and shown up in the
deadline scan as a real bid candidate.

## 4. Signal to keep

PSC 7210 is a recall driver worth keeping - it is what catches bedding buys
whose titles never say "mattress". The cost is periodic false positives like
this one, which triage is the right place to absorb.
