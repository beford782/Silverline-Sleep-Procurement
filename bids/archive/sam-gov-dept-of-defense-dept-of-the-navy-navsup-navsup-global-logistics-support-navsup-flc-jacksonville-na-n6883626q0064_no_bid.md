## 1. Summary

Solicitation for chair and table covers, delivery Pascagoula, MS.
Posted 2026-08-04, due 2026-08-10. Auto-ingested into the active
pipeline with fit 80 off the NAICS 337121 (upholstered household
furniture) + PSC 7210 (household furnishings) match; triaged same
week in the 2026-08-07 digest sweep.

## 2. Disqualifying factors

- **Primary reason:** Sewn slipcovers for chairs and tables are not a
  Continental Silverline product — CSP manufactures mattresses and
  foundations, not loose textile covers.
- **Contributing reasons:** 3-day runway to the 2026-08-10 due date
  left no room to source or subcontract even if pursued; no
  mattress/bedding line anywhere in the notice.

## 3. What would change a future bid/no-bid call

- A NAVSUP FLC solicitation with an actual mattress, foundation, or
  bedding line (this buying office does purchase berthing/lodging
  textiles — worth staying on the channel).
- A deliberate decision to add sewn-cover products to the CSP line,
  which is not currently planned.

## 4. Lessons captured

- PSC 7210 + a furniture NAICS is enough for the auto-scorer to rate
  a non-mattress textile item fit 80 — "covers" titles under 7210 need
  the same title-vs-product check as the shipboard "mattresses" trap.
