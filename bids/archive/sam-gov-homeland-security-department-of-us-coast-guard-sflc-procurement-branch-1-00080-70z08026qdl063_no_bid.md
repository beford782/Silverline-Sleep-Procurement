## 1. Summary

Combined synopsis/solicitation posted 2026-09-02, due 2026-09-05, NAICS 334 /
PSC 7210. Surfaced by the 2026-09-03 SAM ingest (PR #176) at fit 80 and landed
directly in the **active** pipeline.

## 2. Disqualifying factors

- **Not a mattress or bedding buy.** The requirement is 16 ea Siemens SIMATIC
  **KTP700 Basic 7-inch touchscreen HMI panels** (NSN 7025-01-695-1433, P/N
  6AV2123-2GB03-0AX0), with MIL-STD-2073-1E packaging and MIL-STD-129R
  marking. Industrial control electronics.
- **Why it scored 80:** PSC 7210 (Household Furnishings) is **miscoded on the
  notice** - NAICS 334 (computer and electronic product manufacturing) is the
  accurate classification. The fit score came entirely from that bad PSC.

## 3. Decision

**NO-BID.** Closed at triage on 2026-09-04, one day before its 2026-09-05
deadline, so it never surfaced as a false "due within 10 days" item.

## 4. Signal to keep

A reminder that PSC on SAM notices is operator-entered and sometimes wrong.
The relevance gate cannot tell a miscoded PSC from a real one, so PSC-only
matches with no mattress or bedding term in the title or description should
stay cheap to dismiss at triage.
