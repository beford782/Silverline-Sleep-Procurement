## 1. Summary

Combined synopsis/solicitation for the Department of State Permanent
Change of Station (PCS) Lodging Program. PSC 7210 triggered the
ingest, but the program procures lodging services for relocating
personnel, not household furnishings.

## 2. Disqualifying factors

- **Primary reason:** Product-fit — NAICS 721110 (hotels/lodging
  operations); the requirement is lodging SERVICES, not
  mattresses/bedding, so it is outside the Continental product line.
- **Contributing reasons:** Due date 2026-07-13 arrived with SAM
  registration not yet Active, so a compliant offer was impossible
  regardless of fit.

## 3. What would change a future bid/no-bid call

- Nothing — lodging-program operations is not a lane the vendor will
  enter. A furnishings supply subcomponent solicited separately would
  be triaged on its own.

## 4. Lessons captured

- PSC 7210 alone is a weak signal: it appears on lodging-services
  programs. Cross-check NAICS before spending triage time — 721110
  means hospitality operations, not product supply.
