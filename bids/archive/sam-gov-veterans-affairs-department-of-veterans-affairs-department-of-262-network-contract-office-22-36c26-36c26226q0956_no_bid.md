## 1. Summary

Combined synopsis/solicitation for geriatric mattress pumps at VA
Phoenix. Despite "mattress" in the title, the requirement is
alternating-pressure pump equipment (PSC 6515 medical/surgical
instruments, NAICS 339112 surgical instrument manufacturing), not
mattresses.

## 2. Disqualifying factors

- **Primary reason:** Medical pump/equipment scope — not Continental
  Silverline mattress manufacturing/product lane.
- **Contributing reasons:** Very short response window (posted
  2026-07-22, due 2026-07-27) typical of VA medical-equipment buys the
  vendor cannot serve.

## 3. What would change a future bid/no-bid call

- Nothing for pump equipment. A VA solicitation for the support
  surfaces/mattresses themselves (PSC 6530/7210, mattress NAICS)
  would be triaged normally.

## 4. Lessons captured

- PSC 6515 + NAICS 339112 with "mattress" in the title means the
  pump/therapy-equipment side of support surfaces — a recurring VA
  false-positive pattern worth recognizing at triage.
