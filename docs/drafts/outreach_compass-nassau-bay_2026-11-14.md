## Buyer brief

- **Project status:** Live. Margaritaville's own property page says the hotel "is set to open in late 2027" with "229 coastal guestrooms and suites" [1], and lists it as "Coming Soon" [2][3]. CultureMap (Eric Sandler, 2026-08-11) reports, in the reporter's words, that the owner filed a state notice in June to lay off all employees on July 31 "to facilitate the renovations" [4]. A layoff-notice tracker shows that notice filed 2026-06-09 by Remington Lodging and Hospitality, LLC for 65 workers, effective 2026-07-31, typed "Layoff" [5]. I did not open a source that says in so many words that the hotel is closed. No news newer than September 2026 was found.
- **Owner:** Nassau Bay Resorts LLC. Ashford's SEC filing says the sale closed 2025-08-22 for $27 million to "Nassau Bay Resorts LLC, as successor in interest by assignment from Ayrshire Nassau Bay LLC" [6]. Trade articles naming Ayrshire Corp. or Fischer as buyer disagree with this; I trust the SEC filing because it is the seller's legal disclosure. How Ayrshire and Fischer relate to the owner today is unknown.
- **Route: brand program, probably (not proven).** The brand site says rooms have "Margaritaville bedding" [2], and Margaritaville's store sells the "Luxury Firm Mattress by Beautyrest Hospitality" as "the same exceptional sleep you enjoy at Margaritaville Resorts and Hotels", adding the models "are exclusive to our hotel partners and guests' home purchase" [7]. Neither page says Compass owners are *required* to buy it. **Do not email the hotel.** Better route: first learn whether the brand bed is mandatory; if it is not, go to the owner's purchasing agent or designer once one is named.
